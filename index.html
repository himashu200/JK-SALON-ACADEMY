Beauty, Style and Skill — Crafted With Care

JK Salon & Academy is a professional salon and beauty academy located at S.No. 1, Near PAH Restaurant, R S Pura Main Road, Jammu and Kashmir – 181102. We are dedicated to helping our clients look and feel their best, while training the next generation of beauty professionals.

Our salon offers a full range of hair, skin, beauty and grooming services for men and women in a clean, comfortable and friendly environment. We use quality branded products and follow strict hygiene practices so that every visit is safe, relaxing and satisfying.

Through JK Beauty Academy, we provide practical, hands-on training in hair styling, makeup, skin care and nail art. Our courses are designed for beginners as well as working professionals who want to upgrade their skills, start their own salon, or build a freelance beauty career.

  • Experienced and skilled stylists & beauticians
  • Quality branded products and modern equipment
  • Strict hygiene and sanitisation standards
  • Affordable pricing with premium service
  • Practical-based beauty & hair training courses
  • Friendly, personalised attention to every client
